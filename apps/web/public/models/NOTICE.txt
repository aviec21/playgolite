Neural network used by the "Master" level of PlayGoLite
=======================================================

File:      kata-b10c384-int8-v1.onnx
Source:    KataGo network "kata1-tf2-b10c384-s2941M-d5872M" (kata1 run)
Author:    David J Wu ("lightvector") and KataGo contributors
Licence:   KataGo Neural Network License (permissive, MIT-style), see
           https://katagotraining.org/network_license/
Changes:   Converted from the original PyTorch checkpoint to ONNX, with the weights
